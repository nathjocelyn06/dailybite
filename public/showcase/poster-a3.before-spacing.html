About The Work

DailyBite brings your meals, nutrition, fridge, recipes, groceries, and budget together in one place. Powered by its own MCP server, it connects your food data to AI, helping you track, plan, and make smarter everyday food decisions.

How it works

From a photo to a full day, in five steps

Nothing is saved until you confirm it
1
Snap or ask A meal photo, a grocery receipt, or a question in plain language.
→
2
AI reads it Dish, portion and calories from the photo; items and prices from the receipt.
→
3
You confirm The estimate waits as pending. Correct the portion, then say yes.
→
4
One record updates Journal, calories, fridge stock and food budget all move together.
→
5
It gives back What to cook tonight, what expires first, what's left to spend this month.
