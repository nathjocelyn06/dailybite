About the work
Ever find yourself wondering...
  • What should I eat today?
  • How many calories am I actually eating?
  • Where did my grocery money go?

DailyBite answers all three in one place. Snap a meal and it reads the dish and its calories straight from the photo; scan a receipt and the groceries land in your fridge with what you spent. It keeps a food journal, tracks your food budget in won, watches what is about to expire, and suggests tonight's dinner from what you already own. Everything is one shared record, so the same day shows up in Home, Journal, Archive and My progress — nothing is entered twice. And because DailyBite runs its own MCP server alongside the app, Claude can do any of it with you in plain language: log the meal, fill the fridge, plan the week, or tell you how the month is going.
